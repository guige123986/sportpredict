About SportPredict

SportPredict is a free, independent sports data analysis platform that helps sports enthusiasts and analysts understand match probabilities, compare odds across bookmakers, and gain data-driven insights into sporting events worldwide.

Our Mission

We believe that access to transparent, comprehensive sports data should be free and available to everyone. SportPredict was built to bridge the gap between complex bookmaker odds and everyday sports analysis, making probability data understandable and actionable for fans at every level.

What Makes Us Unique

SportPredict combines two powerful data sources that are rarely available on a single platform:

  • Bookmaker Odds: We aggregate real-time odds from more than 20 licensed bookmakers worldwide, including Bet365, Pinnacle, Betfair, DraftKings, and many others. This gives you a comprehensive view of the traditional betting market.
  • Prediction Market Data: We integrate live data from Polymarket, the world largest decentralized prediction market. This provides a crowd-sourced perspective on event outcomes that often differs from bookmaker assessments.

By presenting both perspectives side by side, SportPredict reveals where the two markets agree and where they diverge. These divergences are often the most valuable insights for analysts, as they can indicate market inefficiencies or value opportunities.

Our Tools

  • Odds Calculator: Convert between decimal, fractional, and American odds formats. Calculate Kelly Criterion optimal bet sizes and detect arbitrage opportunities.
  • Probability Analyzer: Visualize implied probabilities from bookmaker odds as heatmaps and probability bars. Identify overround and market sentiment.
  • Odds Comparison: Compare odds side-by-side across 20+ bookmakers. Find the best available price for every outcome.
  • Daily Analysis: Get curated match previews and insights for today games across all major leagues.

Sports We Cover

SportPredict covers a wide range of sports including football (soccer), basketball, baseball, ice hockey, American football, tennis, boxing, MMA, F1 racing, cricket, rugby, and more. We cover major leagues such as the Premier League, La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Champions League, NBA, NFL, NHL, MLB, and international tournaments like the FIFA World Cup.
