Serie A is Italy's top-flight football league, renowned for its tactical sophistication and storied history. Founded in 1898 and restructured into its current format in 1929, Serie A has been home to some of football's greatest tactical minds, from catenaccio pioneers to the modern pressing systems. With legendary clubs such as Juventus, the most successful Italian club with 36 Scudetti, AC Milan with their seven European Cups, and Inter Milan, recent champions under Simone Inzaghi, the league draws substantial international interest and robust betting markets. The season spans 38 matchdays from August through May, featuring 20 teams battling for the Scudetto and European qualification spots, with the bottom three facing relegation to Serie B. Napoli's electrifying 2022-23 title win under Luciano Spalletti broke a 33-year drought and energized the league, while Atalanta's rise from provincial club to European contender has been one of football's most compelling stories. The current season features a fascinating title race with Inter Milan, Napoli, and Juventus all competing at the highest level, while clubs like Roma, Lazio, and Fiorentina battle for Champions League and Europa League places.
