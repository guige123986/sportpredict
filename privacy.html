Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Information We Collect

SportPredict is designed with privacy in mind. We collect minimal personal information:

  • Usage Data: We may collect anonymous usage statistics such as page views, features used, and general interaction patterns to improve our platform. This data is aggregated and cannot identify individual users.
  • Cookies: We use essential cookies to maintain your session preferences (such as language selection, theme preference, and favorite matches). These cookies are necessary for the platform to function properly.
  • Analytics Cookies: We may use Google Analytics or similar tools to understand how visitors use our site. These tools collect anonymous data such as browser type, device type, and general geographic region.

Third-Party Services

SportPredict uses the following third-party services:

  • Google AdSense: We display advertisements through Google AdSense. Google may use cookies to serve personalized ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalized advertising through Google Ads Settings.
  • The Odds API: We fetch sports odds data from The Odds API. No personal information is shared with this service.
  • Polymarket: We display prediction market data sourced from Polymarket public API. No personal information is shared with this service.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • We do not require account registration or sign-up.
  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or contact information (unless you voluntarily contact us).
  • We do not collect financial information or payment details.
  • We do not track individual user behavior across sessions in a personally identifiable way.

Local Storage

SportPredict uses your browser local storage to save preferences such as your selected language, theme (dark/light mode), and favorite matches. This data is stored only on your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third party. You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect any data we process. Our site is served over HTTPS to encrypt data in transit. However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Children Privacy

SportPredict is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete it promptly.
